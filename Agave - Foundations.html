Agave · a theme for Plone CMS

A light, warm, honest theme.

Born at a Plone gathering in Mexico, Agave keeps its origin subtle — present in an agave-green palette and a warm clay accent, never in literal motifs. It's built to be a clean showcase of what Plone can be, and easy to drop in, re-skin, or replace.

Agave green leads

A desaturated, slightly teal green carries the brand. Light backgrounds throughout — nothing dark or heavy — so any institution's photography sits comfortably on top.

Clay warms it up

A single terracotta accent provides the warm Mexican counterpoint. Used sparingly: a button, a link, a content-type marker — never wallpaper.

Content gets identity

The three core types are quietly colour-coded — News in clay, Events in amber, Pages in green — so listings and pages have rhythm.

Clean & customisable

Hanken Grotesk, soft 6px corners, hairline borders. Every colour, radius and size is a CSS variable — re-skin the whole theme by overriding a handful of tokens.
